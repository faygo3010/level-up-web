How it works

01

Open the chat and describe what you want to track. Daily runs, a side project, reading more — anything with a measurable outcome.

02

Each day, tap the check-in notification and sync with the AI. It references your step count, calendar, and past conversations.

FAQ

Common questions

No account required. There is no sign-up, no email, no password. The app runs entirely on your device. Everything is stored in local storage and never leaves your phone unless you choose to export it.
During each chat the app sends your current goal states, recent progress data, and (with permission) device data like step count to the AI. The AI uses this to give contextual, specific responses instead of generic advice.
The app is free and open source. You bring your own Anthropic API key for the AI chat feature. The key is stored locally on your device and used directly — we do not proxy your requests through any server.
Download Expo Go from the App Store or Google Play, then scan the QR code from the GitHub repo. You will be running the app in under 60 seconds. No App Store approval needed.
Nothing. No analytics, no crash reporting, no telemetry. With permission the app can read your step count and calendar — but that data never leaves your device. The only external call is to the Anthropic API when you open a chat.
